I work in natural language processing and human-AI systems in Hyderabad. My work focuses on human-computer interaction, multimodal interaction, and casual AI, with a particular interest in model evaluation frameworks and dataset quality.

Previously, I completed an Executive Post Graduate Diploma in Data Science at IIIT Bangalore and a BBA in IT at St. Joseph's Degree and PG College.

### Core Interests
* **Human-Computer Interaction**: Designing human-grounded evaluation methods for interactive AI systems.
* **Multimodal Systems**: Evaluating interaction patterns across text, voice, and visual interfaces.
* **Evaluation Frameworks**: Building benchmark protocols to test system performance and model quality.
